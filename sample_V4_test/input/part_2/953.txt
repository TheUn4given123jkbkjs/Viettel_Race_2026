 + White blood cell count: 7.500 / μL (Reference: 3,500 - 10,500 / μL)
* Sinh hóa: 
 + Creatinine: 1,2 mg/dL (Reference: 0,6 - 1,2 mg/dL)
 + Urea: 20 mg/dL (Reference: 6 - 18 mg/dL)
 + Glucose: 120 mg/dL (Reference: 70 - 99 mg/dL)
